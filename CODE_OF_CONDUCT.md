# Code of conduct

Most conversations about this project happen in issues and pull requests. These rules keep them useful and friendly for everyone who takes part.

## What is expected

- Be respectful, including when you disagree. Criticise code and ideas, not the people who wrote them.
- Assume good intent. Many people here are new to PowerShell, GitHub or Claude, and questions are welcome.
- Keep feedback specific: what you ran, what happened and what you expected.
- Protect privacy. Don't post anyone's backups, keys, chat contents or personal details, and remove your own from any output before you share it.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory remarks about anyone's identity, background or experience level
- Sexual language or imagery
- Publishing someone else's private information, including anything from their backups, without permission
- Spam, including posts that only advertise unrelated products
- Pushing a point again after the maintainer has asked you to stop

## Where it applies

These rules cover this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and release comments, and any place where you speak for the project.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, contact the maintainer, Harshit Kushwaha, on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/hrshtk/) and include links to the comments or issues involved. Reports are kept private. Security problems go through the private reporting described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Depending on how serious the behaviour is, and whether it continues after a warning, the maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, close issues and pull requests, or block someone from the repository.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
